# BaBot THT validation

- **Base, archived two-layer draft in `../history/PCB/base_2layer_draft/`:** two copper layers, same 16 component positions and three mounting-hole positions as the four-layer reference. KiCad 10 PCB DRC after zone refill on the routing geometry: **0 violations, 7 unconnected items** (U1_SIG, PT04, PT00, U1_S0, U1_S1, U1_S3, and one GND-pour connection). The 99.95 mm outer width remains within the 100 mm prototype size tier. The draft's local CD74HC4067 module footprint uses 1.7 mm pads with 0.8 mm holes. The separate footprint identifier and matching schematic were assigned after this DRC run; repeat DRC before fabrication.
- **Base, retained four-layer reference:** 16 footprints, 110 physical through-hole pads, no SMD pads, 704 tracks and 20 vias. Its last KiCad 10 PCB DRC after zone refill found **0 violations, 0 unconnected items**. The schematic netlist and PCB agreed on all 95 connected reference/pin assignments after normalizing the schematic's local-label prefix; 13 MCU pins are intentionally unconnected. All three mounting-hole locations remain unchanged.
- **Plate in this repository:** routed two-layer board, 62 footprints, 143 through-hole pads and 19 vias. The supplied routed source had no mask layer assigned to its pads; the current PCB and 37 Plate footprint definitions now include `*.Mask`. KiCad 10 DRC after zone refill on the integrated project found **0 violations, 0 unconnected items**. Its 205 schematic-parity warnings are 143 net names differing only by the schematic `/` prefix and 62 footprint names differing only by the `BaBot_THT:` library prefix; every normalized name agrees. Both mask exports contain 143 pad openings, and the drill file contains 162 plated hits. See [`fabrication/plate_tht/README.md`](fabrication/plate_tht/README.md) before ordering. The older unrouted PCB and invalid maskless/drill-less export are in `../history/PCB/`.

The four-layer reference's +5V_SERVO rail is routed on In2.Cu. Its provisional 2 A sizing assumed 1 oz inner copper; specify that weight to retain the assumption, then check actual simultaneous servo stall current. A 0.5 oz inner-layer option may be feasible with lower measured current or a revised power path, but has not been validated here. The two-layer draft has servo power on the outer layers with local widths down to 0.8 mm and has **no validated 2 A current rating**. Electrical DRC cannot certify temperature rise or servo voltage under load. Mechanical fit, module pin-label agreement, trimmer calibration and real-hardware behavior remain to be checked before fabrication. The four-layer Base Gerbers and drills are in `fabrication/BaBot_Base_THT_4layer/`; their export DRC found 0 violations and 0 unconnected items. The Plate outline is 100.0456 × 101.5921 mm, above a 100 × 100 mm price tier, and its 205 inherited schematic ERC warnings are all `endpoint_off_grid` warnings; see the Plate fabrication notes.
