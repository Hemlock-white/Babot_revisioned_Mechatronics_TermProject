# BaBot THT validation

- **Base, archived two-layer draft in `../history/PCB/base_2layer_draft/`:** two copper layers, same 16 component positions and three mounting-hole positions as the four-layer reference. KiCad 10 PCB DRC after zone refill on the routing geometry: **0 violations, 7 unconnected items** (U1_SIG, PT04, PT00, U1_S0, U1_S1, U1_S3, and one GND-pour connection). The 99.95 mm outer width remains within the 100 mm prototype size tier. The draft's local CD74HC4067 module footprint uses 1.7 mm pads with 0.8 mm holes. The separate footprint identifier and matching schematic were assigned after this DRC run; repeat DRC before fabrication.
- **Base, retained four-layer reference:** 16 footprints, 110 physical through-hole pads, no SMD pads, 704 tracks and 20 vias. Its last KiCad 10 PCB DRC after zone refill found **0 violations, 0 unconnected items**. The schematic netlist and PCB agreed on all 95 connected reference/pin assignments after normalizing the schematic's local-label prefix; 13 MCU pins are intentionally unconnected. All three mounting-hole locations remain unchanged.
- **Plate copy in this repository:** two copper layers, 62 footprints and 143 through-hole pads. It remains unrouted. Its previous placement DRC found 0 rule violations and 100 expected unconnected items. The project owner has a newer routed Plate and Gerbers outside this repository; import and validate those files when available.

The four-layer reference's +5V_SERVO rail is routed on In2.Cu. Its provisional 2 A sizing assumed 1 oz inner copper; specify that weight to retain the assumption, then check actual simultaneous servo stall current. A 0.5 oz inner-layer option may be feasible with lower measured current or a revised power path, but has not been validated here. The two-layer draft has servo power on the outer layers with local widths down to 0.8 mm and has **no validated 2 A current rating**. Electrical DRC cannot certify temperature rise or servo voltage under load. Mechanical fit, module pin-label agreement, trimmer calibration and real-hardware behavior remain to be checked before fabrication. The four-layer Base Gerbers and drills are in `fabrication/BaBot_Base_THT_4layer/`; their export DRC found 0 violations and 0 unconnected items.
