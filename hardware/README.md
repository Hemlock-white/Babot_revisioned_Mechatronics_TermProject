# Mechanical working files

`mechanical/BaBot_original_combined.stl` is a byte-for-byte working copy of the downloaded [combined BaBot STL](../original/mechanical/all_hYIYvbA6Sb.stl). No mechanical redesign has been recorded here yet. Compare printed-part clearances with the Arduino Micro, multiplexer module, connectors, and the adjusted Base board outline before assembly.

`mechanical/MG90S_screw_M2.5x0.4x16_pan_slotted.stl` is a printable MG90S mounting screw (2.5 mm × 0.4 mm pitch, 16 mm under head, slotted pan head Ø5 × 1.5 mm). Regenerate or resize it with `python mechanical/make_mg90s_screw.py` after editing the constants at the top of the script.
